About UOK Fee Portal

UOK Fee Portal is a School Fees Management System developed for the University of Kabianga.

The system provides a centralized platform for student registration, fee management, payment processing and monitoring of outstanding balances.

Students can access their fee information, make M-Pesa payments and obtain payment receipts, while authorized administrators manage financial records.
